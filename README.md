# XL125R Chassis Bench

Whole-bike geometry and Pro-Link linkage calculator for Joel's 1982 Honda XL125R rally build.

Open https://blevo66.github.io/chassis-bench in Safari on the iPhone, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. It works offline and keeps your numbers on the phone; use Back up to save them to a file.

Swingarm & linkage calculator, a generic measuring guide for a swingarm and linkage: https://blevo66.github.io/chassis-bench/arm.html. Send that link to whoever is measuring. Their "Send to Joel" message includes a link that opens the bench with the numbers waiting in measuring mode, ready to check and apply.

Rocker & dogbone designer: https://blevo66.github.io/chassis-bench/rocker.html. Put in the swingarm, the frame's shock top and dogbone mounts, the shock and the angle it runs at, plus the travel and rising rate you want, and it designs the rocker and dogbone. It gives the numbers to type into the bench's CRF style tab, and STL files for a full-size PLA mock-up. Its sources are rocker.src.html plus core.js, built with build_rocker.py (in the project's chassis/rocker folder).
