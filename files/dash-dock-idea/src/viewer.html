Docking dash head

v2. The screen and LiPo live in a 17.5 mm head that pulls onto a dock on the nav tower. Two steel locating pins line it up and four pairs of strong magnets hold it. A standard 2 × 16 pogo block in the dock presses straight onto the ends of a long-pin header pushed into the Waveshare's J8 socket, so there's no custom board. The dock keeps the MOSFET board, GPS, 5 V buck and loom. A magnetic cap covers the pins with the head off.

How it works

  • On: offer the head up, the two 4 mm stainless dowels find their holes, and the magnets pull it home against the pogo springs.
  • Off: pull it straight off. No button, no latch.
  • Hold: 4 pairs of 12 × 3 mm N52 discs behind 0.5 mm skins (1.3 mm between each pair). The dowels take all the sideways and up-down load, so the magnets only resist pull-off. My rough estimate is 60–100 N of hold against 15–30 N of pogo push. That's a guess, so do a pull test and a shake test on the first print.
  • Pogo straight onto J8: J8 is a female 2 × 16 socket, so a gold long-pin 2 × 16 header pushes into it. Its pins run back through a printed guide and are trimmed flush with the back face. Those pin ends are the pogo targets.
  • Seal: TPU ring round the pogo block, 0.3 proud, squashed by the head's back face. The v3 TPU seals and shadow lines are kept at both splits.
  • Dock wiring: take the back cover off and the MOSFET terminals face you. The loom comes in through a flush grommet in the back.

Pogo contacts (J8, 2 × 16 at 2.54 mm)

All 32 J8 positions line up with the pogo block. The dock only wires the 18 you need: IN1–8, 5V (VBUS), 3V3, GND × 2, RPM, brake, SDA, SCL, GPS TX and RX. The 2 A light and horn currents never leave the dock.

Pull the unused pins out of the long header, VBAT above all. With the head off, any pin left in is bare on the back face. VBAT is the LiPo, live next to GND, so a spanner across it would short the battery. Pliers push single pins out of a header easily.

Pin ends are small (0.64 mm square), so alignment rests on the dowels (±0.15 mm or so). Use a gold-plated header and crown-tip pogo pins. Tinned pins fret under vibration.

Not modelled: a fail-safe on the dock to keep low beam and tail on with the head off. Say if you want it.
