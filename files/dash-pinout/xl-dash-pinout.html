Corrected 27 Nov 2026 to match Waveshare's official pinout diagram. The first version had odd and even pins swapped: it put SCL/SDA on 25/27, but they are really on 26/28.

Other side of every button to earth. On a breakout board, follow its printed labels instead of the DIP numbers. A0–A2 must be high: the screen board already uses address 0x20. Run SDA and SCL as a twisted pair (or 4-core screened, screen earthed at the dash end only), away from the HT lead. Firmware runs the bus at 100 kHz and reconnects by itself if the controller is unplugged.

17 used, 6 spare. Header TE 776228-1 (vertical, pins soldered to wires), loom plug TE 770680-1. Outputs are on one row, power and sensors in the middle, the controller bus on the other row, so the PWM tail wire sits away from SDA/SCL. Each lamp and the horn take their + from the bike fuse box and their − from the OUT pin. Pin 9/10 alone are fine at a realistic 5–6 A; if you add big loads, double up on spares 14/15. check I've assumed AMPSEAL numbering is 1–8 on the first row, 9–15 middle, 16–23 last. Go by the numbers moulded on the header, not this order.
