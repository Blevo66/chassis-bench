XL125R screen pod wiring

Pod v6, socket out the right-hand side. Everything inside the pod: the Hamadap M12 socket, the Waveshare's J8 header, the GPS and the LiPo. Six jobs come down the cable: 5 V, earth, SDA, SCL, RPM and brake.

Facts come from Waveshare's schematic and the parts we've picked. Anything marked check is my best guess until you meter it.

Before you power it

  • Find J8 pin 1. Look for a square pad, a "1" or a triangle. I can't see which end it is from Waveshare's files. check
  • Meter the Hamadap leads and the cable. Buzz each M12 pin to its wire colour before soldering, and write it down.
  • Never put anything on J8 pin 2 (VBAT). That's the LiPo's own rail.
  • No 5 V from the bike while the USB-C is plugged into a laptop. Unplug the M12 when you're flashing it.
  • RPM and brake arrive as clean 3.3 V-safe signals. The pickup filter and brake opto live in the brain box now, so nothing extra goes in the pod. The ESP's own pull-ups do the rest.
  • Current: screen, Wi-Fi and LiPo charging peak somewhere around 0.5–1 A (estimate). 5 V and earth are each doubled across two pins, so you're well inside the socket's 1 A per pin.
