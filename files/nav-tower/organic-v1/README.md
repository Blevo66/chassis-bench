## Shape
- **Cheeks**, 4.5 mm thick, hug the outside of the clamp at both bolt lugs, then follow the clamp's waist inward.
- A **web** crosses the front of the clamp's middle, where the clamp face is set back.
- A **neck**, 48 mm wide, runs forward through the gap the fork tubes leave at full lock.
- The neck flows into a **pod** with a 5" headlight recess (140 mm rim, 42 mm deep, both placeholder sizes), a **3" dash pocket** (84 x 62) facing you, and a **5" tablet pocket** (154 x 88) on a deck tilted 50° up towards you.

## Clearance (scan ±3 mm, 40° lock from Joel)
- **Tightest spot: 4.3 mm** at the front-left corner of the upper lug, between the cheek and the left fork tube at full left lock. Your clamp itself has only ~5-7 mm there, so this is set by the clamp's lug corners, not the tower. Check the clamp-to-fork-tube gap at full lock on the real bike. If it's under ~10 mm, use countersunk bolts or thinner cheeks.
- Pod and deck vs forks, yokes and bars: 18 mm or more.
- Vs frame: 10 mm or more.

## Facts vs guesses
**Facts:** your clamp geometry from your STEP. The clamp placed so the U-bolt sits where you drew it, leaving ~3 mm to each yoke. 40° lock.
**Guesses:** headlight size, tablet size (generic 5" in a case), and dash module size. Screen tilt. No strength check: the cheeks and neck carry everything, so I'd lay them up thick or bond in an alloy plate where they meet the bolts.
