# Rally nav tower, v1: U-bolt clamp (2026-11-17)

Joel's idea: two U-bolts go round the front of the head tube and clamp back. The tank bosses are left free for the tank.

![views](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Blevo66/chassis-bench/claude/project-thread-a69swg/files/nav-tower/v1/tower-v1-views.png)

## How it holds on
- A **saddle** (curved block) sits on the front of the head tube between the yokes. The neck and tower come forward off it.
- Two **M8 U-bolts** (8 mm rod, 76 mm between legs) wrap round the saddle front. Their legs run back along the outside of the head box, one above and one below the tank boss.
- Each U-bolt goes through a **back bar** (about 96 x 16 x 12 mm) that sits inside the open triangle of the frame behind the head. The bar bears on the front edge of that opening.
  - Lower U: front 74 mm up the steering axis, bar about 136 mm behind the axis.
  - Upper U: front 160 mm up, tilted down to the bar about 152 mm behind.
  - The legs are about 170-190 mm long, so they would be bent from threaded rod rather than bought as stock U-bolts.
- The neck (34 wide, 64 tall) runs forward from the saddle between the two U-bolts, then carries the carbon tower as in v0.
