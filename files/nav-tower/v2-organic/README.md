# Nav tower v2: organic, Carbon Fox style

This replaces v1 (`../v1-3d/`) after Joel asked for something less boxy and more like the Carbon Fox tower.
It uses the same coordinates as the clean frame model:
- Origin: on the steering axis, at the bottom of the head tube.
- X: to the right of the bike.
- -Y: forward.
- Z: up.
- Units: mm.

## What changed from v1
- **Cheeks:** chunky 12 mm moulded carbon cheeks (X ±95 to 107), with flowing spline outlines, three organic windows and 4 mm rounded edges. They frame the nose like the Carbon Fox side plates.
- **Nose:** a lofted rounded cowl (superellipse sections with a slight beak) carrying the twin lamps. The lamps are now 85 mm round lamps at X ±46.
- **Top cradle:** a shield-shaped forged-carbon cradle with rounded edges and the same VESA-100 M6 inserts. The cheeseboard, Tab A9 and dash are as in v1.
- **Visor:** a curved smoked visor.
- **Neck:** rounded in plan, otherwise the same path to the 4 M8 bosses.
