# Rally nav tower, v2: Joel's 55 ID top U-bolt (2026-11-17)

![views](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Blevo66/chassis-bench/claude/project-thread-a69swg/files/nav-tower/v2/tower-v2-views.png)

- **Top mount (Joel's design):** a 55 mm ID U-bolt, 8 mm rod assumed, sits horizontal at scan Z 152. It wraps the bare back of the head tube above the top tube junction. Joel has checked on the bike that there's room, and he's ordered one. The legs point forward through a 12 mm plate on the head-tube front, with nuts just in front of it.
- **Bottom mount:** v1's lower U-bolt round the saddle, back to a bar in the frame opening.
- **Neck and tower:** same as v0/v1.

Head tube at Z 152 in the scan: axis Y 69.9, front Y 39.3, back Y 100.5. A horizontal cut through the leaning head tube is about 54 x 61 mm.

**Caveat:** at the back of the head, the scan is ambiguous about frame vs top yoke, so the clearance numbers right there aren't reliable. Joel's look at the real bike wins. Elsewhere the clearances are as v1.
