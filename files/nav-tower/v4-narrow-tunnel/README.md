# Nav tower v4: narrower monocoque with a tunnelled window

This is v3 (`../v3-monocoque/`) changed per Joel: narrower, with a tunnelled window through it.
It uses the same coordinates as the clean frame model:
- Origin: on the steering axis, at the bottom of the head tube.
- X: to the right of the bike.
- -Y: forward.
- Z: up.
- Units: mm.

## Changes from v3
- **Narrower:** 180 wide overall, down from about 212. The lamps are now 70 mm at X ±38, which sets the nose width. The waist behind the dash is 148, which is as narrow as the 133 wide dash allows with 3 mm walls and a little clearance.
- **Tunnelled window:** the three open side windows are gone. There is now one lined tunnel that goes right through the shell side to side, with a 3 mm carbon wall all round it. It runs up the body parallel to the sloped back face, in front of the dash, about 40 mm wide at the bottom and tapering to 24 at the top.
- **Front edge:** pushed forward 12 to 16 mm in the middle and upper body to make room for the tunnel. The top front is now at f 256.
