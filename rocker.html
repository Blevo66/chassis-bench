Leverage ratio

Wheel movement per mm of shock movement, through the travel. Falling from left to right is a rising rate: the rear gets stiffer as it compresses.

Put in where everything bolts to, the shock and the angle it runs at. It then designs a rocker and dogbone that give the wheel travel and rising rate you ask for. All positions are in mm from the centre of the swingarm pivot, with the bike seen from the right.

Print a mock-up

Makes STL files for a PLA test print on the Bambu, at full size. The rocker is a dog-leg: its pivot boss sits on the swingarm's lug, and its shock and dogbone bosses sit over to the side by the offset below, so the shock and dogbone run straight. Set the holes to suit your bolts and bearings, and allow for the printer's tolerance.

Without the bench, the defaults are its CRF style guesses: CRF450 arm, the XL's own shock top and lower dogbone mount. The page keeps your numbers on this phone.
